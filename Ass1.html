Department Overview

The Department of Information Technology provides quality education in programming, networking, database management and software development.

Students learn modern technologies through practical sessions, projects and workshops.

Vision and Mission

Vision

To develop skilled engineers with technical knowledge and professional ethics.

Mission

  • Provide quality technical education.
  • Encourage innovation and research.
  • Develop problem-solving skills.
  • Promote industry-oriented learning.
